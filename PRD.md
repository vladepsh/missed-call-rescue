# Product brief

## Problem

Locksmith and home-service operators routinely miss calls while driving,
working on-site, or handling another customer. In an urgent category, that
missed call can become a lost job within minutes.

## Product promise

Recover a high-intent missed call quickly while keeping consent, legal risk,
pricing, and dispatch decisions under explicit human control.

## Primary user

A small locksmith or home-service owner/operator who needs a clear recovery
queue rather than a generic autonomous receptionist.

## Core workflow

1. A missed call enters the queue.
2. Consent and do-not-contact state are checked.
3. An approved rescue template is simulated.
4. The customer reply becomes structured intake.
5. Routine work becomes ready for dispatch; sensitive work pauses for approval.
6. An accountable operator approves the handoff.
7. Completion makes a voluntary review request eligible.
8. The session audit trail records each meaningful action.

## Acceptance criteria

- A first-time viewer can understand the demo in under five minutes.
- The normal recovery journey works on desktop and mobile.
- Legal-sensitive work cannot skip the human approval queue.
- Opt-out blocks outreach and cannot be bypassed with approval.
- Review requests are unavailable before job completion.
- Every simulated external action creates a session audit event.
- All displayed people, numbers, and events are clearly synthetic.
- A fresh install can pass syntax, server, security, and browser workflow tests.

## Non-goals

- Live SMS or telephony
- Autonomous pricing or dispatch
- Customer authentication or CRM integration
- Production data persistence
- Legal advice
- Review gating or fake testimonials
